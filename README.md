## Featured

### [Line Down](https://github.com/slmens/line-down)

Watches Toronto's transport network and measures what happens when part of it fails. When the subway stops, Bike Share trips within 250 m of the station rise about **14%** in the first hour (95% range: +10% to +17%). That is real, but small: about 0.7 extra trips per disruption.

Live collectors, a DuckDB and dbt warehouse of 45 million trips, a Dagster pipeline, a difference-in-differences analysis, and an incident-brief agent whose numbers are checked against its own facts. Try the [pixel map](https://slmens.github.io/line-down/) or the [Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/selim.agca/viz/LineDownwhenthesubwaystops/Dashboard1).
